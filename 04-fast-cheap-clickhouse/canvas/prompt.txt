SEASONS: chart='seasons',x='month',y='group_name' (or 'scientific_name'),z='observations'.
Example monthly seasonal pattern for birds, insects and plants in 2025:
SELECT toMonth(observed_on) AS month,group_name,count() AS observations FROM exploration.wildlife
WHERE observed_on >= '2025-01-01' AND observed_on < '2026-01-01'
AND group_name IN ('Birds','Insects','Plants') GROUP BY month,group_name ORDER BY month,group_name.
This displays an annual wheel/seasonal comparison. Month must be 1..12, with the group returned as a column.
